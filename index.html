About JP Hospital

JP Hospital combines pediatric and neonatal care with 38 years of clinical leadership.

JP Hospital, Solan is a pediatric and neonatal care center led by Dr. JP Bisht, MBBS, MD(Paeds), DCH, Consultant Paediatrician & Neonatologist with 38 years of experience. He is the winner of the Himachal ICON Award 2026 and has served as Senior Consultant Paediatrician & Neonatologist in HP Health Services for 23 years and at JP Hospital for the last 15 years.

The hospital provides care for newborns, children, and adolescents with a compassionate, family-centered approach supported by NICU Level 2, PICU, HDU, Modular OT, OPD, EEG, USG, ECG, Digital X-Ray, Laboratory, Pharmacy, and vaccination services for children and adults.

Our Services

Comprehensive Pediatric & Neonatal Services Working Together

NICU Level 2

Advanced care for premature and high-risk newborns including stabilization, monitoring, and respiratory support under expert neonatology supervision.

PICU

Specialized intensive care for critically ill children with round-the-clock monitoring and multidisciplinary pediatric support.

HDU

Comfortable, child-friendly inpatient facilities supported by trained pediatric nurses and family-centered care practices.

Pharmacy

In-house pharmacy stocked with child-safe medications, neonatal formulations, vaccines, and emergency pediatric drugs.

Laboratory

Accurate and timely diagnostic services including neonatal screening, infection markers, metabolic tests, and routine pediatric investigations.

Digital X-Ray, USG, ECG & EEG

In-house diagnostic support with digital X-Ray, ultrasound, ECG, and EEG for timely evaluation and treatment planning.

OPD

Dedicated outpatient consultations for newborns, children, and adolescents, covering routine visits, follow-up care, and illness assessment.

Vaccination for Children & Adults

Vaccination services for children and adults following age-appropriate schedules, preventive care guidance, and follow-up support.
